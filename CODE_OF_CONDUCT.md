# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Atlas aims to be a welcoming community for contributors and users regardless
of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality,
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
Everyone participating in project spaces is responsible for respectful conduct.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels. Give concrete,
constructive feedback; accept corrections; take responsibility for mistakes;
and prioritize the community's well-being. Debate ideas without attacking
people. Respect privacy and another person's request to end an interaction.

Harassment, intimidation, sexualized attention, slurs, discriminatory language,
personal or political attacks, trolling, and publishing private information
without permission are unacceptable. Continuing unwanted contact or encouraging
others to target a person is also unacceptable.

## Scope and responsibility

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, Discussions, and other Atlas
community spaces, and when someone represents Atlas publicly. Maintainers
may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when conduct
violates this policy. They should explain moderation decisions when doing
so would not compromise privacy or safety. Maintainers are subject to this
policy too; anyone involved in a report should not adjudicate their own case.

## Confidential reporting

Send a confidential report to repository maintainers through
[the private reporting form](https://github.com/DEVxEAZY/atlas-cli/security/advisories/new).
For conduct reports, start the title with **Code of Conduct:**. This project
uses that private GitHub channel for conduct as well as security reports;
please do not open a public issue or include the report in a pull request.

Describe what happened, where and when it happened, and include relevant
links or screenshots and your preferred follow-up. Share only the personal
information necessary to investigate. Maintainers will handle reports
discreetly, share details only as needed to respond, and avoid identifying
reporters publicly without consent. This is a volunteer project; there is
no guaranteed response time or emergency response service.

For GitHub-hosted abuse, or concerns involving repository maintainers, you
can separately use [GitHub's Report abuse process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
That is a platform-level report to GitHub, not a private message to Atlas
maintainers. Do not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers consider the impact, severity, and recurrence of behavior, may
seek clarification privately, and can take protective action while reviewing
a report. The response can include:

1. **Correction:** a private explanation and a request to correct behavior or remove inappropriate content.
2. **Warning:** a notice describing the violation and limits on further interaction.
3. **Temporary ban:** a defined period without participation or contact with affected community members in project spaces.
4. **Permanent ban:** removal from project participation for serious or repeated violations.

Serious harm can warrant immediate restrictions without earlier steps.
Appeals or requests to reconsider may use the same private route and should
include new context. Where possible, an uninvolved maintainer will review
them; if none is available, independent internal review cannot be promised.
